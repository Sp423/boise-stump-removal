Stump Grinding in Boise

Stump grinding is the fastest and most affordable way to eliminate unsightly tree stumps from your Boise property. Our commercial-grade stump grinders reduce your stump to wood chips 8–12 inches below ground level — eliminating the trip hazard, preventing pests, and restoring the appearance of your lawn.

The grinding process takes anywhere from 15 minutes to a few hours depending on the stump's size, species, and root structure. When we're done, you'll be left with a mound of wood chips that can be used as mulch — or we can haul them away for you.

  • ✓ Eliminates trip hazards and eyesores
  • ✓ Discourages insect infestation
  • ✓ Wood chips left for mulch or removed
  • ✓ Safe for use near fences, structures & utilities

Root Removal & Treatment

Surface roots are one of the most common complaints from Boise homeowners. Left unchecked, roots from large trees can buckle sidewalks, crack driveways, damage foundations, and make mowing nearly impossible. Our root removal service addresses these problems directly.

We cut and remove exposed surface roots and, where appropriate, treat the root ends to discourage regrowth. This service can often be performed without removing the entire stump — though we'll advise you on the best approach during your free consultation.

  • ✓ Sidewalk and driveway root damage repair
  • ✓ Foundation root protection
  • ✓ Root end treatment to inhibit regrowth

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does stump grinding take? +
Most residential stump grinding jobs take between 30 minutes and 2 hours per stump. Larger stumps, harder wood species (like oak or locust), and stumps with extensive root systems take longer. We can give you a time estimate when we see the job in person.
Will stump grinding kill the roots? +
Stump grinding removes the main stump and grinds the roots near the surface. Over time the remaining roots will naturally decompose underground. For trees prone to sprouting (like cottonwood or locust), we recommend a root treatment to prevent new shoot growth.
Do I need to call 811 before stump grinding? +
Yes — before any digging or grinding, Idaho law requires you to call 811 (Dig Line) to have underground utilities marked. We can help guide you through this process and in many cases handle the notification as part of our service.
Can you grind stumps near fences, buildings, or gardens? +
Yes. We have compact grinders that can access tight spaces such as narrow gates, fenced yards, and areas near structures. Let us know about access constraints when you request your quote so we can bring the right equipment.
What do I do with the wood chips after grinding? +
You have three options: leave the chips to decompose naturally (they make excellent mulch), spread them over garden beds, or have us haul them away entirely. We discuss your preference at the time of service.
