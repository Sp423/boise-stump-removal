Our Story

Built on Honest Work and Community Pride

Boise Stump Removal was founded by a lifelong Boise resident who grew tired of seeing homeowners overcharged for simple stump grinding jobs. With professional equipment, fair pricing, and a commitment to doing things right, we set out to be the stump removal company the Treasure Valley actually deserves.

We've removed thousands of stumps across Boise, Meridian, Eagle, Nampa, Caldwell, and every community in between. We've worked on small backyard jobs and multi-acre commercial clearings — and we approach every single one with the same care and professionalism.

We're not a national franchise. We're your neighbors — people who live, shop, and raise our families right here in the Boise area. That means we're always just a phone call away, we stand behind our work, and we genuinely care about the communities we serve.

Our Credentials

Licensed, Insured & Idaho Code Compliant

Boise Stump Removal holds all required Idaho state and local business licenses for tree and stump removal services. We carry full general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage — protecting both you and our crew on every job.

Before any stump grinding project that involves digging near the surface, we follow Idaho Code by notifying underground utility locators through the 811 Dig Line program. Your safety and the safety of your property's infrastructure is our top priority.
